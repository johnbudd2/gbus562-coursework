### You

Wildframe is a specialty coffee company with a subscription service. We want to reward subscribers who stay six months or longer with new perks.

Generate 6 perk ideas. Make them distinct from each other. Mix tangible items, experiences, and access/recognition rather than six variations of a discount. Each should be realistic for a small coffee brand to deliver.

Format: a numbered list of exactly 6 ideas. Each idea is one sentence of no more than 25 words. No intro or closing text.

### Claude

1. Send a free bag of a rare, limited-release single-origin coffee available only to six-month-plus subscribers.
2. Invite loyal subscribers to a virtual live cupping session hosted by Wildframe's head roaster.
3. Offer early access to new seasonal blends two weeks before public release.
4. Feature long-term subscribers' names or photos on a "Founding Fans" wall on the website.
5. Provide a personalized coffee profile card matching subscribers with brews based on taste preferences.
6. Grant an annual free bag swap, letting subscribers trade any shipment for a different roast.
